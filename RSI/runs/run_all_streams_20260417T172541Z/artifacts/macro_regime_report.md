### Structural Drawdown Risk

Only **10% of months** hurt both regimes simultaneously. These occur when:

1. **Sudden disorderly moves** — market moves fast but without follow-through.
  Neither trending enough for IMBAL (not enough continuation) nor reverting enough for BAL (not enough bounce).
2. **Apr 2025 tariff shock** is the clearest example: BTC dropped 15% in days,
  all long signals stopped out (IMBAL −28R, BAL −31R = −59R combined).
3. The portfolio's only systemic vulnerability is macro shocks that break price
  out of regime cleanly and stay broken — brief, violent, no pattern.
